FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Can you update my current website?

Yes. Send its address and describe the changes. We will assess the existing platform and quote the work.

Will I be able to edit the website?

If you need regular editing, we agree a CMS before starting. A simple static landing page does not include a CMS by default.

Does the price include advertising or SEO promotion?

No. Basic technical SEO is included; ongoing promotion and advertising are separate services.

Who owns the domain and accounts?

We recommend registering them to you. Access and handover are agreed before work begins.
