Where businesses lose customers

Your customer has already reached out.
AI automation helps you keep them.

From slow replies to forgotten follow-ups — we automate the processes that help solve these problems.

01

Slow replies cost you customers

Someone messages in the evening, at the weekend or while your team is busy — and gets no reply. They simply go to whoever answers first.

02

Your team spends most of its time answering the same questions

“How much does it cost?”, “Is there an available slot?”, “When is delivery?”, “What documents do I need?”, “Is it in stock?” — instead of selling, your staff are constantly acting as a help desk.

03

Enquiries are scattered across channels

Instagram, Telegram, your website, phone calls and other channels operate separately. Messages are easy to miss, forget or handle too late.

04

Without consistent follow-up, potential customers disappear

“I'll think about it”, “message me later”, “I'll come back in spring” — and the team forgets to follow up. Often, your business has already paid to acquire that lead.

05

Existing customers are left behind

A customer has already bought from you, had treatment, travelled or taken a course — but no one automatically reminds them about their next appointment, purchase or additional service. This is one of the most cost-effective ways to generate repeat revenue.

06

Human error costs your business money

A team member is busy, off sick, has gone home, missed a message, forgotten a callback or qualified a lead incorrectly — and your business loses money. The risk is especially high at night, in the evening, at weekends and during peak hours.

Setup

How we implement AI automation

From process analysis to launch and monitoring.
Setup takes 3–14 working days, depending on the plan.

01. Business and process analysis

We examine how your business works today: where enquiries come from, how your team replies, how bookings or sales happen and where leads get lost. We identify processes an AI agent can handle.

02. Building and integrating your AI agent

We tailor the AI agent to your business: services, pricing, rules, schedules and conversation flows. We connect the channels, CRM or calendar it needs so it can do more than answer questions — it can take real action.

03. Launch, monitoring and automation

We test the agent with realistic scenarios, resolve issues and launch it. Your AI agent works 24/7: answering questions, qualifying customers, booking and rescheduling appointments, sending reminders and handing over only the enquiries that need a person.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Does AI replace the receptionist?

The agent handles routine questions, contact collection and agreed booking actions. Complex, unusual or sensitive conversations go to a person. Your team controls the rules and can intervene.

Will AI work with our services?

The knowledge base, services, prices, specialists, appointment lengths and booking rules are configured for each business.

Can you connect our CRM?

That depends on its API. Before setup, we need to check access to the schedule and support for creating, rescheduling and cancelling appointments.

Is there a working AI chat on the website?

The website has a demo chat with answers about N&P Agency services, plans and setup. It currently uses prepared scenarios and does not create real bookings. We connect the production AI agent to each business's knowledge base, calendar and CRM. We will show its capabilities in a 15-minute personal demo.

How much does setup cost and how long does it take?

Quick Start — $500, Premium — $850, VIP — from $1500. Setup takes 3–14 working days, depending on the plan.

What needs to be agreed before handling customer data?

Before setup, we need to define access to conversations, data retention and deletion, and the rules for handing a conversation over to a person.
