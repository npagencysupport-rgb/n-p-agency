Privacy & data

What happens to the information you share with us.

Updated 21 October 2026

How we handle your enquiry.

Data contact

N&P Agency website: np-agency.com.ua. For enquiries and personal data requests, contact montellai.support@gmail.com. The service provider’s identity and payment details must be agreed before entering a contract.

What you submit

The form collects your name, contact, selected service and message, plus the page and language of the enquiry. We use these to respond and discuss your project. Do not submit passwords, payment details or health information.

Where the data goes

A Vercel server processes the form and sends it to the team’s private Telegram chat. Authorised team members can access those messages. Vercel and Telegram may process data outside Ukraine under their respective policies.

Retention and requests

We use enquiries to communicate about your task. Contact the email above to request correction or deletion. Information necessary for a contract or mandatory records may be retained on the applicable basis. Customer-data arrangements for AI projects are agreed separately before connection.

Analytics and preferences

Vercel Web Analytics collects visit statistics such as pages, referral sources, countries and device types without third-party analytics cookies. Theme and language preferences may be stored in your browser. GA4 and Meta Pixel are not currently installed.

Demo chat

The demo chat uses prepared replies and does not make real bookings. Do not share confidential data there. Use the enquiry form or contact details to reach us.

External services

Vercel privacy policy · Telegram privacy policy. External websites have their own terms.
