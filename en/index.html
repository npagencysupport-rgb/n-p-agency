FAQ

Your questions, answered.

How do we start?

Choose a service and describe your task in the form below. We clarify your requirements and prepare a scope and estimate.

How long does it take and how do I pay?

Each service has its own timeline. We agree milestones, the final amount in UAH and payment details before starting. Third-party subscriptions and advertising spend are separate.

What do you need from me?

Your business description, goals, available text and images, and access to the services needed for the selected task. Share access through invitations, not passwords in the form.

Is support available after launch?

Yes. Launch corrections and ongoing support are agreed separately. Hosting, subscriptions and further changes are listed in the proposal.
