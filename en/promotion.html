FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Is advertising spend included?

No. The price covers our work. You pay the advertising platform directly from your account; we agree the budget before launch.

Do you guarantee a number of leads?

No. Results depend on demand, competition, your offer and the website. We agree a test plan and evaluate actual data.

Do I need a new website?

Not necessarily. We assess your existing website. A rebuild is a separate decision and is not required to order promotion.

Do you have published marketing results?

We do not yet publish a verified marketing growth case. ShoreGuard is presented as a website development project, without claims about traffic or sales.
